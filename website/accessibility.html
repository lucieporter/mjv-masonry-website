MJV MASONRY & CONTRACTING — ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

FINAL BUSINESS DRAFT — EFFECTIVE AUGUST 12, 2026 — NOT YET REVIEWED BY LEGAL COUNSEL

MJV Masonry & Contracting is committed to providing a website and customer experience that are as accessible and usable as reasonably possible for people with disabilities.

OUR APPROACH

We aim to consider accessibility when designing, updating and maintaining our website, forms, images, documents and customer communications. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we welcome feedback about barriers that visitors encounter.

WEBSITE ACCESSIBILITY

Where practical, the website team should use recognized accessibility practices, including clear page structure, descriptive links, meaningful alternative text for images, keyboard-friendly navigation, sufficient colour contrast, readable text, accessible form labels and understandable error messages. MJV Masonry & Contracting does not claim formal WCAG conformance unless and until the website has been tested and that claim has been approved.

ONTARIO REQUIREMENTS

MJV Masonry & Contracting will follow accessibility requirements that apply to its organization under Ontario law. Ontario's specific public-website WCAG requirements apply to designated public-sector organizations and businesses or non-profits with 50 or more employees. If MJV Masonry & Contracting becomes subject to those requirements, the website and web content will be reviewed and maintained accordingly. Regardless of organizational size, MJV Masonry & Contracting will make reasonable efforts to identify and remove accessibility barriers.

ALTERNATIVE FORMATS AND COMMUNICATION SUPPORTS

If you need information from this website in an alternative or more accessible format, or need a communication support to access information, contact MJV Masonry & Contracting. We will work with you to identify a suitable option based on your needs and the information requested.

QUOTE FORM ASSISTANCE

If a disability or accessibility barrier prevents you from using the online Quote Form, contact MJV Masonry & Contracting using the contact information on the website so that another reasonable method of providing the necessary project information can be discussed. The public website does not provide direct calendar booking; quote requests are reviewed before any appointment or booking link may be offered.

FEEDBACK

Accessibility feedback is welcome. Please identify the webpage, form, document or feature involved and describe the barrier you experienced. MJV Masonry & Contracting will review feedback and consider reasonable corrective action.
