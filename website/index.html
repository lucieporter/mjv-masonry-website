About MJV

Real Skill. Real Experience. Real Results.

MJV Masonry & Contracting was founded by Marty Vandenberg with one straightforward goal: deliver quality masonry work that holds up over time and leaves customers glad they called.

After being in the business for many years, Marty brings deep trade knowledge and craftsmanship to every job.

Years of experience: Approximately 50 years in the trade, ~30 years operating the business.

Qualifications: Business insurance and safety training.

What sets us apart: Better craftsmanship, more experience, restoration expertise, attention to detail, customer service, quality materials, and reliability.
