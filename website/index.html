Expert Masonry Contracting

MJV Masonry and Contracting provides specialized residential and commercial brick, stone, and structural repairs. Marty does not offer quick cosmetic patches—he does structural, heavy-duty work meant to last.

1. Brick Repair & Repointing

Crumbling mortar and spalling bricks invite serious water damage. Marty grinds out failing paths and replaces mortar or bricks cleanly to maintain your building's structural health.

  • Brick repair & replacement
  • Custom brick repointing
  • Color & mortar profile matching

2. Chimney Repair & Rebuilds

High winds and freeze-thaw cycles break down chimneys first. From small brick mortar tune-ups to complete structural chimney tear-downs and rebuilds, Marty handles it safely.

  • Chimney tuckpointing & repairs
  • Complete chimney teardowns
  • Crown repairs & flashing prep

3. Stonework & Custom Masonry

Professional application of natural or manufactured stones for a high-end architectural look. Includes fireplaces, custom brick installations, and complex outdoor living spaces.

  • Natural stonework layouts
  • Manufactured stone veneers
  • Indoor fireplaces & outdoor kitchens

4. Restoration & Heritage Masonry

Historic structures require lime-rich, soft mortars. Using heavy Portland cement on old brick causes catastrophic crushing. Marty understands old-world heritage matching.

  • Heritage-approved lime mortars
  • Careful non-destructive cleaning
  • Historical detail matching

5. Steps, Porches & Walkways

Unstable, shifting front steps are major slip-and-fall hazards. Marty builds heavy stone steps, level walkways, concrete block structures, and reinforced retaining walls.

  • Front steps & porch masonry
  • Structural retaining walls
  • Flagstone & heavy block work

6. Parging, Foundation & Water Repairs

Moisture traveling through crumbling foundations degrades block networks. Marty applies clean sand-and-cement parging coats and seals concrete cracks to keep water out.

  • Smooth decorative parging
  • Concrete block foundation repair
  • Water intrusion & spall protection
